## 🎮 Level-Up: Your Esports Playground

Hey there! **Level-Up** is your go-to platform for all things **esports**. Whether you're a casual gamer or an aspiring pro, we've got you covered. Compete in **free and paid tournaments**, connect with fellow gamers, and level up your skills in a vibrant, competitive community.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🏆 Join and host esports tournaments
- 💸 Participate in both free & paid events
- 🤝 Connect and chat with other players
- 🧠 Skill-based matchmaking (Coming Soon!)
- 🌐 Fully responsive and user-friendly interface
